App settings

Colours

Primary colour affects

  • App name and username greeting
  • Main Save and checkout buttons
  • Selected supplier filters and the cart count
  • Navigation links and sidebar hover/selection highlights
  • Field focus outlines and App edit highlights
  • The sign-in page background
  • The fixed bottom area of the sidebar
  • Sidebar icon outlines

Secondary colour affects

  • Add-to-cart buttons
  • Home quick-link buttons
  • Stock Edit button accents
  • The active App edit button
  • Brand arrow accents
  • Internal lines and details in sidebar icons

Custom text colours and background overrides take priority for the parts you change.
