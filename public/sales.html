Projection & budget

Projected sales = the average of your location’s previous four completed Monday–Sunday weeks. Each projected day uses the same weekday’s sales from those four weeks. Budget = projected weekly sales × 28%.

Weekly sales report

Separate from projections

Record your location’s daily takings and weekly costs. This report is saved separately and does not change daily sales, projected sales or your ordering budget.

Weekly overview

Total sales = Till sales + Uber Eats + DoorDash.

Daily variance = cash + Eftpos + petty cash − till sales.

Leave unrecorded cells blank. Enter 0 for unused sales channels. Daily sales totals need till sales, Uber Eats and DoorDash; variance needs cash, Eftpos, petty cash and till sales.

Cash to be deposited is the cash row total. Stock cost uses orders placed in the selected week. Percentages use recorded report sales.
