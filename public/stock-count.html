Weekly usage

Usage is calculated automatically: Sunday stock count + orders + confirmed transfers in − confirmed transfers out − the next Sunday stock count. All counts and orders from your assigned location are used.

Use the same units or packs when counting and ordering. The latest Sunday count for each item is used, including zero counts.

Weeks run Sunday to Saturday in Melbourne time.

Save your Sunday stock count and place orders as usual. This breakdown and your target levels update automatically.
