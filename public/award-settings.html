Requires separate review

    Award updates

    The uploaded edition is bundled locally. A newer PDF or verified rule update is needed when the award changes. Generic imported checks supplement the native engine; they do not replace its pay tables. A payroll provider is optional.

    Public holiday calendars

    Choose a state or territory in Locations → Edit location to fill public holidays automatically, including past and future years. Forecasts use these dates without annual entry. Review and save a location calendar only for regional exceptions or award cost calculations.

    Edit location state

    Dates include state-wide and substitute holidays. Regional and specially announced dates may need a saved location calendar. Calendar source and licence

    Dated rule sets

    Import a rule set prepared from a verified source, or enter referenced rules below. Imported and saved sets start as drafts. Reviewing a version enables its checks for its effective period; this does not certify full award compliance.

    New rule set

    Amounts and thresholds must come from the source. No legal defaults are pre-filled. Different age, classification and employment bands can use separate rules.
