How par levels work

Par is your location’s average usage over the previous four completed Sunday–Saturday weeks, rounded up to whole units or packs. Usage is calculated from Sunday stock + that week’s orders − the next Sunday stock count. Save Sunday counts and place orders as usual; usage updates automatically. Five consecutive Sunday counts are needed for four complete weeks. Missing counts or negative usage need checking. Recommended order = par − the latest stock count, rounded up to whole units, with a minimum of zero.
