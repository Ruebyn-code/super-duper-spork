Home is a compact dashboard for the selected location. Quarter sales, the quarter expense percentage, all-time placed orders, completed-week quarter averages, the ordering budget, cart and stock count information keep their existing calculations.

To customise it on a computer:

1. Open **App edit** from the blue account dropdown.
2. Select **Customise Home**, or click a Home part and choose **Home layout** in the editor.
3. Choose compact or comfortable spacing, 2–6 desktop quick-link columns, cards or list style, page width, spacing and corners.
4. Show or hide sections, individual totals and budget metrics, quick-link descriptions, date labels and calculation notes. Enable the additional Rostering, Comms, Knowledge Library and Reporting shortcuts as needed.
5. Save changes. Hold and drag sections, metrics and quick links directly on Home to change their order; moves save automatically with Undo.

Hidden cards appear faded while editing so they can be found and restored. The layout options reset keeps wording, colours, uploaded backgrounds, fonts, icons and saved ordering. Those appearance settings remain available by clicking each part in App edit, with individual or linked changes.

Changes are shared within the selected company. Other companies and networks keep their own settings. Layout visibility never grants access: users only see shortcuts they have permission to use. Phones use the saved layout with two quick-link columns and keep App edit hidden.
