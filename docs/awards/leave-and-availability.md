# Availability and leave requests

Regular availability and planned leave must be submitted **14 Melbourne calendar
days before they start**. This is the business's scheduling rule, not a universal
notice requirement created by the award. Leave approval does not replace any
longer statutory notices, agreements, evidence requirements or eligibility checks.
Unexpected statutory absences have an explicit short-notice path with a reason
for review. Sick/carer's, compassionate, family and domestic violence, emergency
service, jury service, unexpected parental and work-injury absences can use it.

Recurring availability has an effective date and keeps its earlier schedules.
Weekly changes, including removing an override, obey the same 14-day rule.
School-holiday schedules have inclusive start/end dates, no notice period, and
override weekly and recurring availability only during that range. Overlapping
holiday ranges are rejected to avoid ambiguous schedules. Approved leave takes
priority over all availability. Overnight windows cannot leak across a holiday
override boundary.

## Source and types

Source: the retained [MA000003 award edition](ma000003-2026-07.pdf), consolidated
1 July 2026. Clauses 3 and 23–27 incorporate or refer to the National Employment
Standards; long service leave and compensation also depend on applicable laws.

| Request / absence | Award reference |
| --- | --- |
| Annual leave, annual leave in advance | 22, 22.3 |
| Personal (sick), paid and unpaid carer's, compassionate/bereavement | 23 / NES |
| Parental, unpaid special parental and pre-adoption leave | 24 / NES |
| Jury service and voluntary emergency management | 25 / NES |
| Family and domestic violence leave | 26 / NES |
| Long service leave | 3.2(g) / NES and applicable state arrangements |
| Agreed time off in lieu | 20.7 |
| Public holiday absence | 27 / NES; not annual leave |
| Work injury / workers compensation absence | 19 and applicable state law |
| Other agreed unpaid leave | Employer agreement; additional to minimum entitlements |

Annual leave cashing out is a payment arrangement rather than time away, so it is
not a roster absence type. Annual leave in advance and TOIL requests still require
the agreements and other conditions in the relevant award clauses.

These remain absence request/review records, separate from payroll payments.
Automatic tracked annual-leave balances now use a known starting point, approved
ordinary hours and confirmed paid absence hours. Approval can prefill supported
hours; missing historical data and unknown hours remain unavailable rather than
becoming zero. Annual-leave provisions and super estimates also appear separately
in roster Costs. See [Automatic annual leave and super](../employee-benefits.md)
for the supported rules, reservations, holiday handling and limitations.

## Access and roster behaviour

All standard roles can request their own leave. Franchisees and Head Office can
review leave at accessible locations; nobody can approve their own request.
Only Head Office has confidential leave review by default. The three leave grants
are individually configurable in permission levels. Existing custom roles do not
silently acquire them.

Family and domestic violence details, notes, short-notice reasons, audit and request
identifiers are omitted for ordinary roster managers and other employees. Generic
unavailable times remain visible for scheduling, without disclosing the type.

Pending leave does not block shifts. Approved leave and leave awaiting cancellation
review block overlapping shifts across the user's locations in the same company.
An availability override cannot bypass approved leave. Existing shifts are kept
and flagged; publication is blocked until they are moved or reassigned, or the
leave is cancelled through review. Cancellation requests continue to block work
until a reviewer agrees. Records retain revision checks and an action history.
