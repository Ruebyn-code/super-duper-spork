# Stock wastage

Ordering → Wastage is a location-specific stock loss log. It uses the company catalogue and normal location selector, including kiosk pinning. Each entry has a past/current Melbourne date, one or more distinct stock items, positive quantities (up to three decimals), a reason for each item and optional notes. Saved counting units such as sleeves are optional; the server validates their conversion and the selected pack before snapshotting native three-decimal ordered quantities. Individual units are worked out automatically by the shared `public/stock-individual-units.js` from the item's saved counting unit, pack description and name: pieces, multiplying every nested pack size down to the smallest count ("Pack of 10 pita" → Pita · 10 per pack, "Box of 8 packs of 10 pita" or "8 x 10 pita" → Pita · 80 per box with Pack · 8 per box offered in between, "2 x 12 x 330ml cans" → 24 each and 7,920 mL, "Box of 1,000 cups" → 1,000 cups; "10 pita per pack" on an item counted in Packs of 8 → 80 pita), and grams/millilitres from a kg/g/L/mL size or a saved weight/volume counting unit ("5kg" → 5,000 g). New lines start in the smallest individual unit (the piece with the most per ordered unit, then g/mL); the cost of an individual-unit line uses its exact share of the price (1 of 80 pita = 1/80), while the saved ordered quantity keeps the native three decimals, so staff record 1 pita or 100 g of chicken rather than a pack; Ordered packs and the saved counting unit remain available. Nothing is guessed when the description has no number ("Box"), and nothing is saved on the item. The server rebuilds the same allowed units and rejects any other unit (409), then converts to ordered packs as usual. Quantities smaller than 0.001 of an ordered pack are rejected rather than silently recording zero.

Estimated costs use the saved stock cost per ordered pack, rounded to cents for each line. A known zero price is valid. A missing price remains unknown, with a labelled known subtotal rather than a zero or invented total. Names, packs, conversions, quantities and cost prices remain frozen after catalogue changes or item removal. Summaries show active-entry costs, entry/item counts, reasons and highest item costs. Different pack descriptions are not merged into one quantity. Date filters use the wastage date, not the recording timestamp, and support ranges up to one year. Search/reason/status filters apply to individual item lines; View entry always shows the complete original entry. CSV exports include all filtered lines, not just the current page, and protect spreadsheet formula text.

Wastage is tracked separately from physical stock count snapshots. It does not subtract from those snapshots again, change usage targets or ordering suggestions, alter carts/orders, or add a second expense to invoice-only weekly Cost of goods. Total physical depletion already includes wasted stock. This version covers stock items, not finished meals or recipes.

Access is inherited from existing stock-count grants, refined by four optional detailed actions: `wastage.view` requires `counts.view`; recording and voiding additionally require `counts.edit` and `wastage.record` / `wastage.void`; CSV requires `wastage.export`. Explicit denial of viewing blocks all wastage endpoints. These controls are editable in Permission levels → Wastage. Original feature grants and customised role maps are preserved. Native company/location access, CSRF and required-reading checks remain enforced. No generation, email or provider calls are involved.

Creates are atomic, serialized and idempotent by actor, context, request UUID and normalized content. Retrying after a catalogue change returns the original snapshot; new stale-pack/conversion entries fail for explicit reload and re-entry. Voiding requires an explicit reason and matching revision. It preserves original details and the recording actor, adding the void actor/time/reason. Void records remain available through status filters/export and are excluded from totals; there is no hard deletion or implicit correction.

The company-styled page is registered with the persistent shell, required reading, layouts, Ordering subpages, platform page search, optional Home shortcuts and the shared floating cart. Phone forms retain touch-sized inputs and scrolling dialog bodies with fixed actions. Drafts are scoped to network/company/location/user in browser storage, retained on close and guarded during navigation. Before a write, freeze the request for uncertain retries; do not permit editing/discarding until its outcome is known. Stock reload clears quantities whose pack/conversion changed. Reads and exports never write operational records.

The adjacent info icon opens the written/picture/video guide in Help & support. Tests use isolated synthetic locations, accounts and stock, including concurrent retries, restarts, unit/price changes, unknown costs, void audits, denied actions, company/location access, formula-safe exports and unchanged counts/expenses.

## Smart insights and recording help

`GET /api/wastage/insights` (wastage viewing, this location's recorded entries only; voided entries ignored; read-only) feeds `public/wastage-smart.js`:

- **This week so far** compared with the average of the same weekdays (Monday to today) in the previous four Monday–Sunday weeks, with an 8-week known-cost trend. Unpriced items are noted as partly unknown.
- **Wasted again and again**: items wasted on three or more days in the last 28 days, with days, known cost and a tip chosen from their most common reason (`wastageModel.tip`: e.g. Expired → order smaller amounts or lower the target level; Overproduction → make less on quieter days). Tapping one searches the log. Tips are suggestions only and never change targets, orders or prep amounts.
- **When waste happens**: known cost by weekday over the last 28 days and the busiest day.
- **Recording help**: up to eight Frequently wasted chips (last 28 days) add the item to the form; choosing an item fills its usual reason (most common in the last 8 weeks) when no reason is chosen and shows a "Usually n" quantity placeholder; a warning appears when a pack quantity is over three times the usual (with at least three past entries) and when the item was already recorded today. These only fill the open draft; saving still uses Save wastage.

## Recording form

Each line in Record wastage is one card: the stock item, a How much row (− / + stepper that moves by 1, or 100 for grams/millilitres, unit pills smallest first with the ordered unit last, and the line cost), a short conversion line ("2 pita = 0.025 box", or "1 pita ≈ $0.50" before a quantity is typed) and Why chips for the reason. The native unit/reason selects stay hidden underneath (`data-native-select` keeps the shared dropdown from wrapping them), so drafts, saving, validation and the smart hints are unchanged; Save names a missing reason and focuses its chips. Changing the unit keeps the typed quantity. Display only — the saved data and server rules do not change.
