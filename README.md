# Souvlaki Gr ordering

A stock ordering application with a searchable catalogue, supplier filters, editable quantities, and saved purchase requests. Prices are in AUD. The app starts with sample stock; submitting a request does not change inventory or place an order with a supplier.

## Run

Requires Node.js 22 or newer. No external packages are required.

```sh
npm start
```

Open port 3000 in your development environment. Set `PORT` to choose another port.

```sh
npm test
```

Requests are saved as individual JSON files in `data/` (ignored by Git). Totals are calculated by the server from catalogue prices. This is a local prototype with Head Office and ordering accounts. Supplier orders use configured email sending; saved stock counts provide ordering guidance. Request files contain contact details; keep the data directory private.

## Suppliers and stock

Sign in with a Head Office account, then open **Suppliers & stock → Suppliers** and select **Add supplier**. Enter a supplier name and select **Save supplier**. Open **Stock items → Add new stock**, choose that supplier in the stock form, enter the item name, price in AUD, then select **Save stock**. SKU, category, and description help organise your items. Use the supplier buttons above the catalogue to view their stock.

Below the stock editor, each supplier has its own collapsible menu with an item count, including suppliers with no stock yet. Expand a supplier to see its stock. Current and newly added items appear under their assigned supplier. Menus start collapsed and retain their open or closed state while you save changes. Select the pencil beside a stock item to expand its form directly underneath it and update its name, supplier, SKU, category, price, photo, or description, then select **Save changes**. **Cancel edit** leaves the item unchanged. Inside its editor, select **Remove stock** to delete an item from the catalogue. Remove all a supplier’s stock before removing the supplier. Previously saved requests retain their original item names, supplier names, and prices. Supplier and catalogue changes persist in `data/catalogue.json` across restarts. Keep the entire `data/` folder when replacing the application with a downloaded update.

Everyone must sign in. Ordering accounts can browse stock and submit requests; Head Office accounts can also manage suppliers and stock on the separate Administration page. Stock quantities are informational; submitting a purchase request does not adjust them automatically.

## Accounts and permissions

On a fresh installation, start the app with `npm start` and open it on the same computer at http://localhost:3000. The app shows first-time setup: choose a Head Office username and a password of at least 12 characters. Once an account exists, first-time setup is locked. Existing Head Office and user accounts from earlier versions are preserved automatically.

Sign in with a Head Office account and open **Administration → Users**. Choose **Ordering manager**, **Franchisee** or **Head Office**. Ordering managers can order, enter sales and save stock counts. Franchisees can do the same and create, edit or remove users at their assigned locations. Head Office has full access, including all active locations, location email, suppliers, stock and app settings. Select **Create user** at the top of the Users list to add a user. Select the pencil beside a user to edit their username, permission level, allowed locations, default location or password directly below their row. Leave the password blank to keep it. You can remove accounts, but at least one Head Office account must remain. Changing a user’s location access, default location, credentials or permissions invalidates that account’s active sessions immediately. Sessions also expire after eight hours or a server restart.

Franchisees can only assign **Ordering manager** or **Franchisee**, and every location on a new or edited account must be one of their own. They cannot view or edit Head Office accounts, or users shared with locations outside their access. They cannot manage locations, sender email, suppliers, stock definitions, app settings or App edit. Ordering managers cannot manage users. All checks run on the server as well as controlling the menus; forged requests and options cannot assign a higher role. Changes to role or location access revoke the affected account’s existing sessions. At least one Head Office account must remain. Head Office sees and manages all users and locations.

Account data is stored privately in ignored `data/accounts.json`, with salted scrypt password hashes. Keep the entire `data/` folder when updating. Legacy `admin.json` and `users.json` are read only until the unified account file has been saved; thereafter `accounts.json` is authoritative.

For forgotten Head Office passwords, stop the server, run `npm run admin:set` in the app folder, then restart with `npm start`. This local recovery command resets the named account to Head Office access. `npm run user:add` remains available for creating or resetting ordering accounts. Normal account management happens within the app. Existing stored `admin` and `user` roles keep their IDs for compatibility and now display as Head Office and Ordering manager; the new `franchisee` role sits between them. Passwords, memberships and historical data are retained.

Use locally on your Mac; an Internet deployment requires HTTPS and appropriate hosting configuration.

Head Office lists use compact rows with a pencil to reveal edit controls. Removal actions stay inside the editor. User creation is a button at the top of Users rather than a separate submenu. Suppliers, stock, locations, sender email, colours, fonts, logos, sign-in backgrounds and interface text use the same inline editing style. Add buttons reveal their forms; existing supplier stock remains grouped in collapsible supplier sections. Earlier custom menu wording and saved appearance edits are retained.

## Logo

With a Head Office account, open **App settings → Logo**. Upload a PNG or JPG (up to 2 MB) under **Sign-in / app logo** for sign-in and setup. Under **Sidebar logo**, upload a separate image for the very top of the sidebar, above the location name and cart. Wide transparent PNG images keep their original proportions. Preview your selection before saving. **Use app logo** restores the sidebar to the sign-in/app logo. These settings persist in `data/logo.json` and `data/sidebar-logo.json`; keep the entire data folder when updating. Refresh other open pages to see changes.

Add or edit stock photos in **Administration → Suppliers & stock**. Choose a PNG or JPG up to 2 MB, then save the item. Photos persist in the catalogue and appear on ordering cards. Use **Remove photo** and save to clear a photo. On-hand quantities are no longer shown or entered.

## Spreadsheet catalogue

This release includes the 128 items and 11 suppliers from Book2.xlsx. The catalogue merges them once on first access after updating. Existing matching items and suppliers are preserved rather than overwritten. Imported edits and deletions are retained on subsequent starts. Keep your existing `data/` folder.

The spreadsheet provides 106 prices; 22 items have blank prices and display **Price pending**. Enter their prices by editing the stock items. Orders containing pending prices show the subtotal of known prices plus a pending-price notice. Pack sizes and item names follow the spreadsheet; prices are rounded to the nearest cent.

## Home

After sign-in, every user opens **Home**. The greeting appears first, followed by **Location totals**, which shows recorded sales for the current calendar quarter, the all-time value of placed orders and **expense percentage = current-quarter placed orders ÷ current-quarter sales × 100** for the selected location. Quarter dates use Melbourne time: January–March, April–June, July–September and October–December. Each day in a sales week is counted separately, so weeks spanning a quarter boundary are split correctly. The quarter labels update automatically. Unsaved carts and catalogue stock value are excluded. Partial sales weeks contribute only their recorded amounts; location sales corrections replace the original totals. Pending prices on current-quarter orders or zero quarter sales leave the percentage unavailable. Older pending order prices affect the all-time order display only. Home buttons use a light tint of the primary colour. The dashboard greets you by name and shows the selected location, Melbourne date, stock catalogue size, your location-specific cart, latest shared stock count and weekly ordering budget. Quick-access buttons open Ordering, Cart, Stock count and Sales in one row. They keep their existing desktop/phone height; scroll the row sideways on smaller screens. Head Office also sees administration shortcuts; franchisees see Users. Use **Home** in the sidebar or phone navigation, or select the logo, to return. The ordering catalogue keeps its existing `/` address; Home is at `/home`. Data refreshes on focus and periodically; cart changes in another tab update the dashboard. Switch locations beneath the logo to load that location’s dashboard.

## Ordering and cart

Ordering uses a simple page title without the personal greeting. The ordering catalogue shows 25 items per page. Use Previous/Next to browse; searching or choosing a supplier resets to page one and filters the complete catalogue. Select **Cart** at the top of the left sidebar to review quantities and submit a request on its own page.

Cart items are grouped under collapsible supplier headings with item counts. Select a supplier heading to hide or show its compact rows; your expanded/collapsed choices stay in place while quantities change or another tab adds stock. Each row keeps its quantity, remove controls and line total together. All suppliers, including collapsed groups, are included when submitting. Carts are saved per signed-in account and assigned location in this browser’s local storage. Successful orders remove the submitted quantities, keeping any additions made while the request was saving.

Select a supplier in the horizontal menu to filter the catalogue; scroll it sideways when needed. The small search icon beside **Menu / List** smoothly expands into a search field when selected. Select its close icon or press Escape to collapse it and clear the search while keeping the chosen supplier. Search and supplier filters work together. Navigation and submenus use compact icon rows; the collapsed sidebar is 64 pixels wide. Stock cards, budgets, forms and admin records use consistent spacing and lighter panels, with two-column account/stock/email forms on larger screens. Head Office views display only the selected panel. Existing colours, uploaded fonts, logos, wording and individual appearance edits still apply. Search expansion respects reduced-motion preferences.

Date editors in stock counts, weekly usage and sales use the same compact expanding style. Select the calendar icon or its label to reveal the date field; use the close icon or Escape to collapse it while keeping the selected date. Existing date validation, stock-count drafts and Monday/Sunday week handling still apply. Required invalid date fields open automatically for correction. Expansion respects reduced-motion preferences.

Head Office users can change primary/secondary colours and choose fonts for body text, app name, greeting, headings, stock names, buttons, labels, inputs, and navigation in **Administration → App settings**. Choices save in `data/appearance.json` and apply across pages, including sign-in. Refresh other open pages after saving. Upload additional fonts under **App settings → Fonts → Font uploads**. TTF, OTF, WOFF and WOFF2 files up to 2 MB are supported, with a preview before upload. Each uploaded font becomes available for every text group and for individual elements through App edit. Uploading does not change the current font until you choose it and save. Fonts persist in `data/fonts/` and `data/fonts.json`; keep the entire data folder when updating. Re-uploading the same font reuses its existing entry.

## Locations, users and email sending

Open **Administration → Users → Locations** for a searchable location directory showing each location’s users and store email. Use **Filters** and removable location chips to narrow the list. **Add location** opens the creation form at the top. A location’s pencil opens its name, archive/restore controls and email sending settings below the row. The same layout becomes compact cards on phones. **Users** shows one row per user, with initials, name and role, all allowed locations, permissions and a pencil editor. Use **Search people** to find names. Open **Filters** to choose one or more locations; users with access to any selected location appear once. Remove a location chip or use **Clear all** to reset locations and search. The header shows the total user count, and the list shows the matching count. Status is not displayed. On phones the table becomes compact cards with the same controls. In a user’s pencil editor, tick all their allowed locations under **Location access**, then choose their **Default location**. The default must be one of the allowed locations. Users need at least one location. The same controls are available when creating a user. Ordering managers and franchisees enter sales/counts and place orders for their selected location; Head Office also manages locations, users, supplier stock, app settings and email sending. Head Office can switch to every active location without being separately assigned to it. Click the location below the sidebar logo to choose another allowed location. The switcher also works in the collapsed sidebar using its location pin. Switching refreshes the current page and uses that location’s data and order email, preserving separate carts and drafts. It waits for an order or sales/count save to finish. Existing open tabs keep using the location they display, so switching elsewhere cannot send or save their data into another location. Opening a page or refreshing loads the selected session location; signing in again starts at the default location. Location access changes require the affected user to sign in again, and removed access is rejected on the server.

Users at one location share saved sales, stock counts, weekly usage, par levels, recommendations and the ordering budget. All orders placed at that location contribute to usage and committed spending. Each user keeps a separate cart and unsaved drafts for each location. Shared data refreshes when a page is opened or focused and periodically while relevant pages remain open. Different locations remain separate. Head Office users retain access to every location's stock-count history.

Only Head Office users can edit store email under **Locations → pencil → Location email settings**. Set that location’s verified sender email, Resend API key and automatic sending switch. Users have no email editing controls or links, and there is no separate Email settings menu. Keys are masked; a blank key keeps the saved key. Archived locations show their saved configuration read-only until restored. Every user assigned there sends supplier orders through that location's setup. Credentials persist privately in **data/location-email/**; the app displays whether a key exists and leaves the key field empty. A blank key keeps the saved one. Supplier email addresses are managed under **Suppliers & stock**, and each supplier receives only its own items. Orders include the location name and requesting user. Email remains the only supported delivery method and requires a verified Resend sender, credentials and supplier contacts.

Existing single-location assignments are automatically retained as one allowed location, with passwords, roles and history unchanged. On the first update to locations, each existing account receives a location named after its username. Its role/password and existing sales/count/order history are preserved, and its email settings are copied to that location without replacing existing location settings. Head Office users can rename these locations and move users into shared ones. Existing records remain with their original location when their author moves or is removed. Previous account-email and sales files are retained; original sales are read as that location's history until corrected sales are saved in **data/location-sales/**. **data/locations.json** stores names and the original history associations. Existing browser carts/drafts are copied to their original location once; moving to another location uses a separate cart/draft.

An unused location can be **Archived** and later **Restored**. Remove it from every user’s allowed locations first; archiving keeps its data and email settings. Keep the entire **data** folder across updates and restarts.

Supplier emails use the subject **Stock order for [location name]** and list item names and quantities, the requesting user, location, order reference and notes. Prices, pending-price messages and totals are omitted from the email. Prices remain available in the app for budgets and saved orders.

Orders save before sending and report submitted, not configured, failed, or unconfirmed per supplier. Submitted means the email service accepted the request, not confirmed receipt. Unconfirmed submissions are not automatically retried. Repeated identical checkout request identifiers do not send again, and an old request cannot be retried under another location. Older account-addressed email API URLs remain read-only and reject changes; old email page bookmarks open the corresponding location’s settings. Supplier order delivery continues to use the selected location’s sender.

## Automatic weekly usage and par levels

Save a **Stock count** dated Sunday and place orders as usual. The app calculates each location's usage automatically:

**Sunday count + quantities ordered from Sunday through Saturday − next Sunday's count = weekly usage.**

All orders placed on the opening Sunday count toward that new cycle. Orders on the next Sunday count toward the following cycle. Order dates use Melbourne time, including daylight saving changes. Each saved order is counted once, even when checkout is retried. If several Sunday counts include the same item, its latest saved count is used; partial counts do not erase other items counted that Sunday. Zero counts are valid. Non-Sunday counts are available in count history and do not feed this calculation.

Open **Stock count → Weekly usage** for the automatic breakdown: Sunday stock, orders, total stock, next Sunday stock and stock used. Choose a Sunday-starting week to review historical or current calculations, search, or select a supplier. Calculations update from the saved counts and orders, including corrections; no separate weekly usage entry is needed.

Ordering cards and list rows show **Par**, the location's average usage from the available completed Sunday–Saturday weeks within the previous four weeks, rounded up to whole units/packs. With one usable week, par uses that week; with two or three, it averages those weeks; with all four, it uses the normal four-week average. Two Sunday counts are enough for the first cycle; five consecutive Sunday counts provide four cycles. Missing item counts or a negative result leave that cycle incomplete and show what needs checking; they are not assumed to be zero. The current partial week and other locations' history are excluded. Hover over the par panel for the exact average. The four-week window rolls forward automatically. With zero usable weeks, Head Office can open **Suppliers & stock → Stock items**, edit an item and use **Manual par → Save manual par** for the selected location. Enter a whole quantity including zero; blank removes it. Manual par is shared within that location and is automatically superseded as soon as a completed usage week exists, including a week of zero usage. The saved value remains a fallback if all four weeks later lack usable history. Manual values persist in **data/location-par/**; include this folder when updating. Par values do not automatically add items to the cart.

Saved counts and order files remain the source of truth. Previous manually entered weekly usage files and browser drafts are retained but are not used by the automatic calculation. Keep the entire **data** folder when updating.


Ordering items show **Par**, **Stock count**, and **Recommended order** together in both Menu and List views. The count is taken from the assigned location's most recently saved stock take, including stock takes on days other than Sunday. Its date appears above the catalogue. Items omitted from that latest stock take show **Not counted** rather than borrowing a value from an older take.

**Recommended order = max(0, par − stock count)**, rounded up to whole units/packs. A zero count is valid; a missing count, or no usage history and no manual par, leaves the recommendation unavailable. Recommendations use the counted stock directly. You choose the quantity to add to the cart. Saving a newer count updates the displayed counts and recommendations automatically without overwriting entered quantities.

## Sales and ordering budgets

Every signed-in account can open **Sales** in the navigation and enter or edit daily sales in AUD. Sales weeks run **Monday–Sunday in Melbourne time**. Select any date in a week, use Previous/Next week, or return to This week. Blank days are unrecorded; zero records a day with no sales. Partial weeks can be saved. Unsaved entries are kept in this browser per account, location and week. **Reload saved week** discards that week's draft after confirmation. Concurrent edits are detected so another tab's saved sales are not silently overwritten.

**Projected weekly sales = the average sales total over the four completed Monday–Sunday weeks before the current week. Ordering budget = projected weekly sales × 28%.** All seven days must be recorded in each of those four weeks before a projection is available. Each projected day's sales average the corresponding weekday from those weeks. The Projection & budget view shows the source weeks and provides buttons to enter missing sales. Correcting a past week recalculates the projection. The current partial week and other locations' sales are excluded; weeks roll forward automatically. Monetary calculations use cents and round the projection and budget to the nearest cent.

**This week so far** appears in Sales and the cart, and compares entered daily sales with the forecast for those same weekdays, through today in Melbourne. It shows actual sales, expected sales, and the dollar and percentage difference (actual minus expected). Blank days and future days are excluded; zero is a recorded day. Earlier missing days are flagged. Current-week changes update the comparison as you type and are labelled unsaved until saved. Viewing another week keeps the current-week comparison on screen. The cart uses your location’s saved current-week sales and refreshes when sales are saved in another tab or when you return to the page. A projection requires four complete previous weeks; percentage difference is unavailable when expected sales are zero.

Sales, ordering, and the cart show the weekly budget, orders placed by all users at this location during the current Monday–Sunday week, and the remaining budget. Ordering and cart pages subtract the current cart as well. Budget warnings do not prevent ordering. Orders with pending prices leave the remaining amount unavailable, rather than treating unknown prices as zero. Saved requests count toward committed spending regardless of the supplier email sending result. Retries of the same saved request are counted once.

Shared sales persist in **data/location-sales/** under the location's ID and survive restarts, location renames and user reassignments. Previous **data/sales/** files are retained as historical data for their original locations. Keep the entire **data** folder when updating. Labels, fonts, and colours use the existing App edit controls and app theme.
